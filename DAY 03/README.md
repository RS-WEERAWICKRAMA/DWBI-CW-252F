## 📊 Business Intelligence Decisions & Insights (DWBI Conclusion)

Based on the multidimensional analysis of the 2020 Advanced Level dataset, we can derive the following data-driven decisions:

### 1. Strategic Resource Allocation (Stream Popularity):
**Insight:** The Arts and Commerce streams hold the vast majority of student enrollments compared to the Science and Technology streams.
**DWBI Decision:** The Ministry of Education should proactively allocate higher budgets for Arts and Commerce teaching faculties, infrastructure, and syllabus development. Conversely, a strategic campaign should be launched to incentivize enrollment in Physical and Biological Sciences to fulfill the country's future technological and medical workforce demands.

### 2. Academic Support Interventions (Z-Score Analysis):
**Insight:** The Z-Score boxplots indicate significant variances in performance across different streams. Science streams tend to show different competitive distributions compared to Arts.
**DWBI Decision:** Educational boards should implement targeted academic support programs (e.g., remedial classes, better study materials) specifically tailored to the streams showing lower median Z-scores or higher failure rates, ensuring standardized educational quality across all streams.

### 3. Demographic Policy & Gender Gap:
**Insight:** The demographic distribution reveals a heavily skewed gender ratio, with female candidates significantly outnumbering male candidates (approx. 193k females vs 142k males).
**DWBI Decision:** Policymakers need to investigate the root causes of the male dropout rate before the A/L examinations. Strategic decisions should involve introducing vocational training early on or implementing retention programs aimed at keeping male students engaged in the higher education system.
